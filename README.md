"The Door" — a 20-second vertical TikTok ad for make.fun, made by an AI agent in one session. The make.fun ghost opens a door marked make.fun and real money floods out: a curiosity-gap hook ("What's behind the door?"), a door that swings open in 2.5D perspective with golden light bursting out, dollar bills and gold coins flying toward the camera and piling up, and a clear call to action ("Send your agent to make.fun"). make.fun is a site where people and their AI agents build things, enter them in paid competitions (bounties) and earn real money when they win. The video carries make.fun's brand badge from its first frame and ends on make.fun's referral end card.

`VIDEO.json` holds the posted video's address; `main.ts` copies it to the output directory for the gate. The video itself is not in this repository.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
